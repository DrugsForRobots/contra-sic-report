Where The Record Limits, Contests Or Contradicts The Special Investigatory Committee's Report On Angela McArdle & Freedom Calls

The LNC adopted its Special Investigatory Committee's report on June 9, 2025, 11-2-4. This page sets each point where the record limits or contests that report beside what the report says, and, for balance, the points where the record backs it.

Built from the project's checked findings, not from new allegations. Naming a person at a step records their part in the documents, not their fault; nothing here finds that anyone did anything wrong, and no court has ruled. Each point is labelled Record (a document shows it), Claim (someone says it) or Inference (a reading of two or more documents together). Every quote on this page was checked against its source.

Part 1

The Money Figure

The SIC's central number is that Freedom Calls raised $2,468.57 against $49,600 paid. These points bear on how that number was reached.

C1
Limits The SICRecord

The $2,468.57 Is A Same-Month Estimate, Not A Matched Result

The SIC's own account: the two databases could not match any documented donations from Freedom Calls LLC. The figure credits any gift made in the same month as a call to that number, which the SIC calls an extremely generous accounting of the data, and it concludes the amount should probably be calculated at $0.00. Neither $2,468.57 nor $0 is a count of gifts the calls produced; both rest on a match that found none.

Sources

  • SIC Report, June 9, 2025 Minutes, Page 71
C2
Contests The SICRecordClaimInference

The Audit Used Zoho Data, But CiviCRM Took The Gifts For About Half The Calls

Garcia's audit matched the exported data from Zoho for January 1, 2024 to January 31, 2025 against Kixie calls. Freedom Calls was paid from February 9, 2024. The LNC's holding account for incoming gifts was named 1501 Undeposited Funds - civi through July 2024, and on April 7, 2024 the CTO's update said he was still getting payment Pages created in Zoho. Staff said on August 24, 2024 that every contact and contribution that was in Cy is now in zoo (Claim). Inference: if that holds, the Zoho export included the CiviCRM-era gifts; nothing shows the moved records kept the donor phone numbers and dates the match needed. Only the SIC's working files can say.

Callers On The LNC's Own Software; The LLC's Costs Never Examined

By The SIC's Measure, About $34,000 Was Overpaid

Malagon's audit expected that a fundraising contractor would be expected to complete approximately 52,000 calls during that time frame, against 13,738 made. C27
Adds ContextRecordInference

The Record Shows A Poor Contract, Not Money Taken For Nothing

Freedom Calls invoiced month by month at the offer-letter price, took a $200 discount for prompt payment, and refunded $4,000 when a payment failed; its callers made 13,738 dials on the LNC's own system. Inference: that pattern fits an overpriced, under-performing contract with a related party better than the embezzlement some members and writers alleged. If the callers were paid near the SIC's market rates, the margin kept by the LLC would be roughly $31,000–$34,000 over the year; no record shows the actual split. The SIC report never uses the word (C12).
