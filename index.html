Where The Record Limits, Contests Or Contradicts The Special Investigatory Committee's Report On Angela McArdle & Freedom Calls

The LNC adopted its Special Investigatory Committee's report on June 9, 2025, 11-2-4. This page sets each finding where the record limits or contests that report beside what the report says, and, for balance, the findings where the record backs it, in the order the events happened.

About & Disclaimer

Built from the project's checked findings, not from new allegations. Naming a person at a step records their part in the documents, not their fault; nothing here finds that anyone did anything wrong, and no court has ruled. Each finding is labelled Record (a document shows it), Claim (someone says it) or Inference (a reading of two or more documents together). Every quote on this page was checked against its source. The findings run in the order of events, in seven parts; where the SIC report numbers its own findings, this page says "the SIC's Finding".

Finding8
Adds ContextRecordClaimInference

A Budget Line Paid Freedom Calls, But The Related-Party Rule Was Never Applied

The rule adopted 12-0-2 on October 1, 2023 says a contract with a closely related party shall be disclosed to the LNC prior to execution and shall be approved by a 2/3 vote of the Executive Committee or a majority vote of the LNC. No minutes record either step for Freedom Calls. The 2024 budget's fundraising line Includes Fundraising Contractor and fundraising employee, written when that contractor was Padgett himself; the payments fit inside General Fundraising ($143,619 spent of $208,000 budgeted through November 2024). Weir (Claim): It was a budgeted expense. Inference: a budget line caps spending; it is not the approval the rule asks for. The payments were budgeted and reported to the FEC under the vendor's name; what went undisclosed was the owner. That fits neglect as well as concealment, and the record does not settle which.

Part 3 · 2024–2025 · Findings 15–20

The Kennedy Victory Fund

The 2024 joint fundraiser with Team Kennedy, and the money the SIC traced around it.

Finding15
Adds ContextRecord

The Kennedy Victory Fund Raised $9.7 Million; The LNC Kept 10% Of Its Share & Passed The Rest Through For Kennedy

The SIC: Executive Committee votes to participate in Joint Fundraising Committee and McArdle signs Joint Fundraising Agreement (JFA) with Team Kennedy. The fund (C00883751) registered July 19, 2024 and terminated in May 2025. It took in $9,732,438.78 from individuals, $3.6 million of it in January–June 2025, and transferred $8,540,383.02: $3,981,726.46 to the LNC, $3,507,501.43 to 14 Libertarian affiliates, $639,069.27 to Team Kennedy, $139,000 to California's American Independent Party, and about $91,000 each to Libertarian Unity, Mises PAC and Libertarian Mutual Aid PAC. The LNC's books show $398,173 in JFA Commissions, 10% of what it received. The rest was carried as owed to the fund (The LNC reduces what it owes the KVF when it pays bills of vendors on behalf of the KVF.) and, with the affiliates' pass-backs, reported as $6.34 million of coordinated spending for Kennedy, August 2024 to October 2025. $478,104.96 is still on the books.

It notes The SIC made no finding of a diverted opportunity. The record behind it: the SIC said costs would exceed $49,600 – the maximum damages the LNC could reasonably expect to win, and the petition seeks more than $250,000 but not more than $1,000,000. The headline's framing, a Multi-Year Harassment Campaign, is the outlet's.
